A practical starting point

Make your first Tessera project.

Tessera is a browser-based 3D art studio. It uses the brightness and colour information in an image to build a layered relief that you can inspect and export.

01

Getting started

Open the app and drop a PNG or JPEG into the upload area. Tessera creates a live preview as you work. Images with a clean subject and strong tonal separation are a good place to begin.

TipUse “Remove background” when the edges of your subject need to stand out from the print base.
02

Dimensions & slicing

Set the print width and length, then choose the maximum height and base thickness. Layer height controls the vertical resolution; grid resolution and mesh triangles balance detail against processing time.

03

Colour layers

Choose how many extruder colours your project uses, then adjust each layer’s colour and start height. Match extracts a palette from the uploaded image; Auto distributes layer heights as a starting point.

For more control, open the matching settings to tune palette size, hue priority, minority-colour protection, reuse, and filament-library matching.

04

Jigsaw splitting

Enable Jigsaw Split when a project is larger than your print bed or you want a modular wall piece. Set rows and columns, then tune clearance, randomisation, waviness, and pin offset. Use the ratio lock to keep a regular grid.

05

Preview & export

Orbit the 3D preview to inspect the relief from every angle. Layer navigation lets you examine individual colour bands. When the model is ready, use Save Project or export OBJ/STL for the next step in your print workflow.

06

Projects & account

You can use the editor as a guest. Sign up or sign in to save projects and access your account settings, preferences, activity, and filament inventory.
