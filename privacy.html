Privacy Policy

Tessera is built to keep your creative work under your control. This policy explains what is processed when you use the app, what is stored when you choose account or cloud features, and what is handled by our service providers.

Last updated: 9 August 2026

1. Local-first use

Tessera can run locally in your browser on your computer. Images used in the editor are processed locally for preview, slicing, colour matching, and export. If you do not sign in or choose a cloud feature, your images do not need to leave your device and Tessera does not retain them.

2. Accounts and login information

If you create an account, we process the login information needed to authenticate you and provide account features. Passwords are encrypted or securely hashed by the authentication service and are not available to us in readable form. Authentication credentials and tokens are protected in transit and at rest; we do not use login information for advertising or AI training.

Account information is used for sign-in, subscription administration, account settings, support, and related service records. You may request account deletion at any time through the app or by contacting the service operator.

3. Images, projects, and AI

We do not actively store or train AI systems with images uploaded to Tessera. Images are processed to provide the features you request. If you choose to save a project to the cloud, the project is stored so that you can access it across sessions.

You can delete saved cloud data at any time. When deleted, we do not retain copies or backups of that project, subject to a short technical period needed for secure deletion, legal obligations, or disaster-recovery systems operated by our infrastructure providers.

You are responsible for images you upload to the cloud. Do not upload sensitive personal information, confidential business material, content you do not have permission to use, or illegal, abusive, exploitative, hateful, violent, infringing, or otherwise harmful content.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Tessera does not retain or save your full payment card details or other payment credentials. Stripe may process payment, billing, tax, fraud-prevention, subscription, and invoice information under its own privacy policy. We may receive limited service information such as subscription status and invoice references so we can provide paid features and support.

5. Service providers and security

We may use hosting, authentication, storage, analytics, email, and payment providers to operate Tessera. These providers receive only the information needed for their service and must protect it according to their contractual and legal obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

6. Your choices and rights

You can use Tessera locally, avoid cloud saving, delete saved projects, sign out, and request deletion or correction of account information. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, portability, restriction, objection, or complaint to a privacy regulator.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Tessera changes. The updated version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Tessera service operator through the support channel provided with your account.
